2. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, and server logs tied to your IP address and user agent. Some technologies do not use browser cookies but still involve network requests to third parties (for example, loading a font or CDN script).

3. Essential technologies

4. Google Fonts
